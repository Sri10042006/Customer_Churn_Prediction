Tittle:
Customer Churn Prediction

problem statement:

-> If  the Companies lose customers when they stop using their products or services .Then it was leads to financial loss in  their revenue genration. 
-> Identifying customers who are likely to leave before they churn is difficult when there are many customers and multiple factors influencing their decisions.
-> The project aims to use historical customer data to identify patterns associated with churn and predict whether a current customer is likely to leave.

Objective:

-> Build and evaluate a machine learning classification model that predicts whether a customer is likely to churn based on their historical and behavioral information.

AI/ML Type:

-> AI/ML Category: Machine Learning

-> Learning Type: Supervised Learning

-> Problem Type: Binary Classification

-> Target:Churn
